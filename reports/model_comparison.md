# Model comparison: LightGBM, XGBoost and CatBoost across three months

Generated by `fraud compare-models` from the training and validation months only; **the test month (May) is not read**. The frozen model and policy are unchanged.

Each fold trains on every month before the one it scores and scores that month once. Early stopping and calibration use the last 14 days of the fold's training window. Each library gets a grid of four settings, the same 452 features, the same weighting and a learning rate of 0.1; its setting is the one with the best mean PR-AUC over the three months. Money is the cost of the same untuned expected-loss policy for every model: review whenever the expected saving is positive, within the daily capacity, so no threshold is tuned on the month being scored.

- LightGBM's PR-AUC is higher than XGBoost's in all 3 months, by 0.006 to 0.021.
- XGBoost is cheaper than LightGBM in 1 of 3 months; the difference changes sign (+$6,704, -$14,347, +$10,027), so these months do not separate it from LightGBM.
- LightGBM's PR-AUC is higher than CatBoost's in all 3 months, by 0.003 to 0.027.
- CatBoost costs more than LightGBM in all 3 months, by $1,128 to $46,886 (0.4% to 14.0%).
- No library is cheaper than LightGBM in every month, so the evidence supports keeping it; its fast exact SHAP reasons (the last column) are a further reason.

Month to month, LightGBM's PR-AUC runs from 0.523 to 0.556; that spread is the yardstick for any gap between libraries.
